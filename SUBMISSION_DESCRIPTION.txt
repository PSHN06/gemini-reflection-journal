Gemini Reflection Journal is an AI-powered personal sanctuary that transforms introspection into an insightful dialogue with your future self. Powered by Google Gemini, the platform delivers grounded, non-clinical reflections, content-aware auto-titling, and an interactive 3D Emotional Landscape that maps emotions, thoughts, and themes across daily, weekly, and monthly horizons.

The core breakthrough is Temporal Tapes: future-self reflections sealed behind objective psychological conditions rather than arbitrary dates. Under the strict principle "Models Propose, Deterministic Code Decides," Gemini extracts cognitive telemetry while a zero-dependency mathematical engine verifies unlock criteria.

Built with React, TypeScript, and Express, all data is secured via Firebase Authentication and isolated within Cloud Firestore. Intended for containerized deployment on Google Cloud Run, it sets a new standard for private, verifiable personal growth.
